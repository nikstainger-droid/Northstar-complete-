Offline & install

Checking connection…

Open the app once online before relying on offline use. After an update, close and reopen the installed app once while online. This version stores data in this browser on this device.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Background push notifications are not included because this app has no notification server.

Privacy & storage

No account, tracking, advertising, analytics or cloud sync. Your entries stay in local browser storage on this device. Storage is not encrypted; do not save passwords, card details or bank login information.

Local storage status

Data management

Restore replaces current data. Erasing cannot be undone without a backup. Your current Northstar starter data will be imported automatically when it is detected.

How this is built

Northstar is a static, single-user Progressive Web App. It works without a paid service and needs no login. The app is designed for manual planning, not automatic bank access or reliable scheduled alerts while closed.
